# Internal review of the revised MinPI study

## Third reading — independent scientific and baseline review

This review used the revised manuscript, the stored selection records and fold-level scores. The constructive question is whether the choice of restoration masks can hide a failed deletion under a fixed predicate. Selection on 14 PMLB tasks and three official challenge validation sets addresses a separate question about held-out prediction.

| Concern | Check or objection | Required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Originality | The signed two-feature condition translates detection into a classical completely separating system. The cardinality bound is Spencer’s. Block deletion, subdivision and a one-deletion stopping condition all have precedents in delta debugging. The original MinPI threshold game gives a signed marginal for adding a feature. Here several resamplings vote on metric tolerances for deleting a support. | Attribute both precedents, define the changed vote, and state precisely what the new connection predicts. Compare search strategies under the same predicate. |
| Context dependence | The explicit regression predictor realizes the proposed failure, including the equality boundary, under balanced panels. The proof characterizes that margin model. A higher-order exception at any omitted mask defeats a universal interpretation. Matching the number and sizes of random masks leaves restoration frequency per feature and rank-to-signature assignment uncontrolled. | Compare the two entire designs at the same budget. Report the realized coverage in actual folds. A change in a vote when the identity changes establishes dependence of the decision on the mask. It does not identify the cause of this dependence in the trained predictor. |
| Real mechanism replay | Of 26 endpoint-approved proposals, 14 fail one or more reserved-row probes. Nested, separating and random designs flag 1, 10 and 11 of these, respectively. Among the remaining 12 their additional rejections are 1, 3 and 3. Additional masks and rows were used to define a sampled outcome; proposals came from previously explored search paths. | Include both types of disagreement and the conditional denominator. “No observed failure” has finite-probe meaning. Causal or population sensitivity/specificity claims would exceed the evidence. |
| Comparative baselines | Full fitting, independently sized Gain, sparse logistic support and native RFECV are credible controls. Equal-width Gain receives the width from another selector. Its timing omits the cost of choosing the width. RFECV uses fold-local imputation and the same downstream learner. It gets three inner folds, a 20% elimination step and a fresh common refit. It optimizes AUC across widths. Compact Gain also uses local conventions for width search. | Report independent and conditional comparisons, their objectives and their costs explicitly. Present RFECV's better accuracy and reduced speed, and full fitting's competitive speed. Cite the actual ddmin variant if it enters the paper. |
| Statistics | I recomputed both contrasts directly from the 462-row primary results. Equal task weights give AUC differences +0.00245974 and −0.00052897 (separating minus nested and random). The wins/ties/losses are 9/4/1 and 5/7/2. Two-sided exact unadjusted sign probabilities are 0.021484375 and 0.453125. Holm gives 0.04296875 and 0.453125. One task, Sonar, contributes +0.02601 and −0.02018. | Interpret signs conditional on the named cohort and exchangeable directions. An adjusted p-value near 0.05 does not establish a large benefit. One random family per fold and repeated validation cases on the challenge limit inference. Leave the three challenge datasets separate. |
| Verification | A final pass of all tested contexts and a veto for every admissible single deletion are checkable finite properties. Neither rules out a beneficial block change or a failure on an untested restoration. A reference-valued masked prediction and a refitted reduced model can disagree. Lean uses an abstract ballot; the perturbed margin lemma uses rational arithmetic. | Retain the model construction, omitted-mask counterexample, exact scope of the replay and the division between a proof and an empirical check. |
| Release and ethics | The development cohorts include medical and financial measurements. The PMLB subset contains duplicated feature vectors. Identity, patient, household or chronological dependence cannot be excluded by exact-vector grouping. The public challenge validation labels are distinct from hidden competition test labels. The 14 pinned PMLB metadata records lack dataset-specific licensing fields. | Explicitly limit the use case to retrospective methodology. Name what is released and its origin. Avoid claims about consent, anonymity, clinical utility or application-specific fairness that the archive cannot substantiate. Distinguish split allocation using labels from feature selection using labels. |

### Independent inspections

1. I enumerated every ordered pair of eligible tail columns against the recorded masks. In the 41 folds containing such a pair, mean fold coverage is **8.6068% nested, 100% separating and 84.2736% random**. The respective numbers of folds with complete coverage are 0, 41 and 8. In 33 random folds different columns were restored different numbers of times. The excluded fold has fewer than two tail columns and remains in the predictive analysis. All 126 design–fold records, including the empty denominator, are in [the enumeration](audit/rounds/restoration-coverage.csv). Recompute with [the script](audit/rounds/restoration_coverage.py) against the selection receipts.
2. I rendered the paired-effect figure, checked its axes and units against the underlying score differences, and inspected both panels. The horizontal marks span the three observed fold differences. They are descriptive ranges. Many task means coincide exactly with zero. The positive Sonar contrast with nested masks and its negative contrast with random masks are visible on a common scale.
3. For the signed model the bounds imply zero or negative margin when both features have the same inclusion status, and a strictly positive margin when only the first is restored. A row with first feature present and second absent is therefore necessary and sufficient for that orientation. Distinct equal-weight signatures attain the cited bound. The regression witness has candidate MAE 1 throughout, while the anchor MAE is zero only for the useful feature alone. This example tests the operational ballots. The synthetic finite-sample experiment uses squared error as a separately specified loss.
4. In the PMLB runner the outer split is stratified, class encoding is determined on fitting rows, all supports are stored before final outer prediction, and all fresh refits use the same development rows. In the RFECV source an imputer and XGBoost form a scikit-learn pipeline under the native selector. The recorded 3.44 seconds includes RFECV's fitted estimator and a separate common refit. A different final-estimator reuse policy could alter this number. The independently tuned L1 classifier and post-selection tree are also distinct estimators. In the challenge study logistic refits share a training-fold univariate screen. The raw and screened full predictors make the role of this screen visible.

The literature checks used [Shi and Zhao's original game](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33885), [Spencer’s separating-system result](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021980070800382), [Zeller and Hildebrandt's paper](https://pm.st.cs.uni-sb.de/papers/tse2002/?lang=en), [Zeller's complement-reduction implementation](https://www.debuggingbook.org/html/DeltaDebugger.html), [the RFECV documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.feature_selection.RFECV.html), [Bommert et al.'s benchmark](https://sfb876.tu-dortmund.de/en/PublicPublicationFiles/bommert_etal_2020a.pdf), [the original NIPS challenge](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2004/hash/5e751896e527c862bf67251a474b3819-Abstract.html) and [Demšar's cross-dataset comparison paper](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v7/demsar06a.html).

## Fourth reading — after the added solver comparison

The new cross-check holds the predictor, anchor, panels, tolerances and restoration family fixed while changing the solver or the order of tests. I independently reduced [the 126 fold records](../results/context_study/schedules/scores.csv). The direct and recent-veto schedules made 1,494 proposals each. They return the same columns on all 42 folds. Moving the last nonempty veto behind the empty-context test changed total distinct mask counts from 3,304 to 3,004 and deletion-only time from 4.088 to 3.639 seconds. Thirteen folds saved masks, 27 tied, and two used more. The schedule preserves conjunction results with fixed ballots. It does not guarantee savings in every fold. At these absolute times, deterministic computation counts are the more transferable measurement.

The complement-only ddmin control made 1,597 proposals and used 3,360 masks. It retained the same support on 30 folds. Its mean AUC and accuracy were 0.93271 and 82.35%, compared with 0.92808 and 81.89% for direct search. It used fewer masks than direct search in 23 folds. Sonar alone gained 0.06666 AUC, compared with a mean difference of −0.00014 across the other 13 tasks. The new data give a reason to treat the search order as an experimental factor. This is Zeller's documented complement-reduction variant. The older paper also describes a variant with subset and complement tests.

The revised introduction names the estimand and the uncontrolled properties of the random design. The PMLB result now measures actual pair coverage, discloses the largest task effects, and treats independently chosen and supplied widths separately. The discussion describes the medical and fraud evidence as retrospective. I asked the authors to distinguish charged time from mask construction in the challenge experiment, to make the common RFECV refit cost explicit, and to compare with [Carter et al.'s instance-level sufficient input subsets](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/carter19a.html).

### Repeated random masks

The next revision added five fixed family seeds in every original fold. I [recomputed AUC from pairwise ranks and accuracy from the winning class](audit/rounds/random_family_replay.py). The maximum absolute discrepancy over all 210 AUCs and 210 accuracies was 2.22 × 10⁻¹⁶. The script checks original held-out row order and outcomes, support containment, both endpoint masks, mask uniqueness and the cardinality of every mask. Coverage averaged 86.2207% over the 205 design–fold combinations with eligible ordered pairs.

All five task-mean AUCs for the random families lie above the separating result. They span 0.92896 to 0.93074, compared with 0.92808 for separating. The five-family average is 0.93005, with 82.04% accuracy at 37.32% retained. The separating design reaches 81.89% accuracy at 37.89% retained. Sonar contributes −0.03446 AUC to its task-specific contrast with the random average. Removing Sonar changes the equal-task mean difference from −0.00197 to +0.00053. These additional draws characterize random-design variability under the same data, splits, rankings and model seeds. The inferential unit for the prespecified contrasts remains the dataset.

In the revised sources the comparator section separates native RFECV fitting (3.22 seconds) from shared refit and prediction (0.22 seconds), the challenge cost paragraph names the excluded mask construction, and the related work compares instance-wise sufficient subsets with a common support voted on by several panels. Independent Lean runs of `ContextContrast.lean` and `WorkingFamily.lean` completed. The former invokes ordinary Lean axioms for classical logic and quotient types; the omitted-mask lemma and the three working-family lemmas reported no axioms.

### Matched per-feature restoration frequency

The authors responded to the confounding concern with a further, explicitly exploratory comparison on the same 42 folds and three additional seeds. One control permutes which ranked features get the original signatures. The second proposes 2,048 incidence-preserving switches per fold and seed. For two masks it selects one column from each set difference and exchanges them. The two row sizes and the two column frequencies therefore stay fixed. A switch may be refused. There is no demonstrated uniformity over matrices with the observed margins.

Running [the independent verifier](audit/rounds/random_family_replay.py) with `--fixed-margins` reproduced all 252 AUCs and accuracies to a maximum absolute error of 2.22 × 10⁻¹⁶. I checked every realized row size, each feature's restoration count, the endpoints, uniqueness of the masks and the support identities against their allowable feature set. A permutation retained the entire signature multiset. At equal feature frequencies, two different signatures have the same size and cannot contain one another. The only missed orientations arise when two features receive identical signatures. Explicit pair enumeration agreed exactly with the sum of ordered collisions over signature groups in all 252 cases.

All 123 eligible permuted families covered every pair. Of 123 eligible switched families, 32 achieved complete coverage. Their mean coverage was 95.93% (97.07% when weighting all ordered pairs equally). For each control, 27 out of 126 resulting sets of masks were identical to the original up to row order. Fixed assignment, permutation and switching gave mean AUCs of 0.92808, 0.92980 and 0.93114 at retained fractions of 37.89%, 37.38% and 37.43%. One of three permutation seeds had lower suite-average AUC than fixed assignment. All three switch seeds had higher suite-average AUC. Omitting Sonar changes permutation minus original to −0.00093 and switch minus original to −0.00009. This control holds marginal inclusion counts fixed, while the particular combinations of columns can still differ in many ways. These results resist a causal attribution of predictive gains to pair coverage alone.

### Conventional selectors at matched widths

I checked every row in the [supplemental benchmark](../results/context_study/matched-selectors/per-fold.csv) against the separating selector's width and recomputed equal-task means. All 84 widths matched. ANOVA scored 0.91892 AUC and 79.84% accuracy. RFE scored 0.92872 and 81.31%, compared with 0.92808 and 81.89% for separating. RFE had lower task-average AUC than separating on nine tasks and higher AUC on five. Differences from ANOVA or the Gain prefix consequently cannot be generalized to RFE. RFE and ANOVA receive all original columns, an externally supplied width and the outer development rows for selection. MinPI relies on an internal fitting/ranking split and an anchor computed from Gain. The comparison fixes final width, test rows and downstream predictor. Differences may include the effects of these other procedural choices.

### Scientific disposition

I would support submission to a specialist feature-selection, trustworthy evaluation or applied machine-learning venue under the narrow mechanism-and-measurement claim. The principal empirical result is that replacing a nested set of masks can expose additional vetoes. Whether deterministic coverage improves on random queries in applications remains open. The benchmark’s ordered-pair guarantee holds under the directed margin assumptions; the real-data study does not estimate the frequency of this model. Independent Gain, full prediction and RFECV reveal the accuracy cost of additional compression. An across-the-board performance claim or a broad top-tier methods claim would require stronger prospective evidence of added practical value at comparable compression and computation.
