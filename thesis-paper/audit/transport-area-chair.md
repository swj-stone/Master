## Decision on the manuscript reviewed

**Reject at a top general ML evaluation track in its present form.** The strongest result concerns the dependence of the marginal value of interior masks on how a proposed deletion was obtained. On 400 encountered proposals the interiors intercepted 11 additional failing refits at the cost of rejecting 20 passing ones. On 379 drawn proposals the corresponding counts were zero and nine. Seven of the 11 detections came from Sonar. This warrants a focused transport study. It does not establish a portable rule for buying useful information before refitting a model.

**Venue fit.** NeurIPS Evaluations and Datasets, under *Reproducibility, Auditing, and Stress-Testing of Evaluations*, is a defensible target for a revised negative evaluation. Its published 2026 criteria reward controlled, independently verifiable findings that expose a limitation of established evaluation practice. A community using a test-time masking score as a cheap proxy for a reduced predictor is the relevant audience. A result restricted to one bespoke voting selector has weaker significance. A future edition's requirements and dates would need to be checked separately. [Official review criteria](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsReviewerGuidelines)

## Findings that change the decision

1. **The reference prediction is too weak for the question posed.** The introduction asks what votes add when width *and Gain ranking* are already known. Section 5.3 uses the deletion fraction and spline terms. This predictor necessarily ties all proposals at the same width. The paper never asks if the new signal beats the sum of the removed features' normalized Gain values, or the magnitude of the loss on the empty endpoint before thresholding. The latter is already computed from the same validation rows and can order candidates even when all four panels dissent. The Gain tie-break in Section 5.4 tests one nomination rule and does not fill this gap in risk prediction. Compare width plus removed Gain, then add continuous endpoint margins, then add interiors or the worst vote, with the same regularization, training tasks, support pool and refit labels.

2. **The proposal-distribution effect has two plausible explanations that the estimates combine.** Path and drawn sets differ in sizes, composition and selection by prior votes. Seventy-five identical fold–support pairs occur in both. Neither the observed sign reversal nor the two separate within-source leave-one-task-out fits tells a reader how a predictor trained on one proposal policy fares under the other. Fit policies on development tasks only. Evaluate each policy on both generators on omitted task families. Show overlap in width and deleted Gain; compare sources over their common range, report excluded mass, and keep identical fold–support pairs together. If the apparent reversal disappears after matching, the finding becomes dependence on candidate mix. If it persists, it motivates a test on new task families. The measured rates remain valid descriptions of the original collections.

3. **The budget experiment measures benchmark decisions, with very few informative changes.** At the primary half-width budget the minimum is zero for all nominees on 32/40 eligible folds. One New Thyroid fold accounts for the improvement in compression, while extra breaches occur on two other tasks. The fresh Optdigits case repeats the zero-score outcome for all 24 candidates. HAR and gas-drift evaluate final supports by different criteria. A gain in conditional ranking AUC on 28 mixed-outcome folds does not resolve whether users obtain more acceptable compression for their refit budget. A locked audit should report the number of decision changes, ties, guard admissions, outer breaches and paired utility differences on the same folds. A negative result is scientifically informative if its uncertainty excludes a practically material gain agreed before scoring.

4. **The theory is sound within a narrow observation model.** The two indistinguishable populations yield optimal reduced AUCs of 0.99 and 0.50. Thus any estimator given their identical population mask-score table has worst-case absolute error at least 0.245. Likewise, if a randomized breach decision vetoes with probability q, its worst error probability is at least max(q,1−q) ≥ 1/2. These deductions concern an observer restricted to those metrics and a population-optimal reduced rule. They do not quantify error for XGBoost trained on finite samples with access to the original columns. The completely separating construction detects Equation 2, a pattern contradicted by all 226 testable observed proposals on each panel sample. The paper should foreground the empirical test of its coverage assumption, and give the empirical proxy a control using the same features, rows and estimate of loss.

5. **The neighboring literature gives a stronger comparison class.** Hooker et al. established retraining on removed inputs as an evaluation strategy. Hase et al. compared five removal mechanisms and examined out-of-distribution counterfactuals. Gao et al. approximate reduced neural predictors by linearization, and Sun and Raskutti use limited learning after masking in boosting and neural networks. Their theory has architecture restrictions, including symmetric weak trees for the boosting result. The present paper can contribute a classification-specific test of whether inexpensive signals predict joint AUC/accuracy tolerance events on adaptively proposed column blocks. For a broad efficiency claim, include a limited-update or low-fidelity refit comparator and measure actual cost. A small fixed number of fresh boosting rounds is a useful low-fidelity control and should be labeled as such. ## What would make a serious submission

Before examining the next outcomes, freeze the task inclusion list and family groupings, proposal pool, learners, independent-unit splits, continuous endpoint comparator, primary loss, aggregation weights and stopping rule. A defensible primary experiment is paired task-weighted log loss for the enriched baseline with and without interiors under transfer to a fixed deployment proposal policy. Preserve continuous distance to the breach threshold as a sensitivity endpoint because outer test folds supply noisy labels. Report every task, including constant-outcome and complete-tie cases. Use the same time allowance or disclose the additional time and fits for each method. The existing material can support retrospective comparator and overlap checks immediately.

For **serious submission**, the focused question, strongest cheap controls, reproducible measurements and an interpretable prospective result matter even if the votes lose. For a **positive acceptance recommendation** I would need a contribution of broader consequence. Examples are an independently reproduced mechanism that predicts when the masking proxy fails, or a useful region of prospective net benefit over the stronger baseline whose uncertainty and task dependence are visible. A controlled and sufficiently precise negative result covering multiple established masking scores could also meet the track's significance criterion. A wide interval spanning meaningful gain and loss remains inconclusive. These are review judgments, not venue-imposed numeric gates.
