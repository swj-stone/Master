# Independent reviews of the observation and nomination experiments

The frozen PDFs for readings 7 onward are in `packages/transport-review-drafts` inside the evidence release. The reports are internal simulated reviews. Each reviewer chose a disposition. The original outcomes on the historical and UCI panels had already been examined when the new contrasts were proposed.

| Reading | Objection or check | Change and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | The cold reviewer gave a weak rejection for an evaluation paper. Observing the unmasked score could identify a test row in a known dictionary. Varying the receiver alone also left the original candidate construction intact. | Measured unmasked-only, whole-row transpositions and score precision, then compared five feature-type recodings matched on code frequency. Replaced the Gain-generated slate with ANOVA and repeated the local candidate draw three times. [Cold review](robustness-cold-review.md) · [Theory review](robustness-theory-review.md) |
| 8 | Both reviewers supported the evaluation contribution subject to corrections. They queried who knows the random codebook, which restorations change with the ANOVA tail, and what the across-design interaction can support. | Reading 9 specifies the code map and marginal occupancy match. The across-design change and its source-paired interval are printed for each panel. Changing the pipeline shifted the observed mean effect by −5.67 and −4.18 percentage points, with both intervals crossing zero. [AC report](robustness-r8-ac.md) · [Mathematical review](robustness-r8-theory.md) · [Source-level recomputation](robustness_r8_contrasts.py) |
| 9 | The first two reviewers verified the revised PDF. A separate cold reviewer rescored 721 opposite-decision witnesses and checked the full versus ordinal contrast by preserving every strict vote preference. All three supported the narrowly scoped evaluation paper. | The additional comparison reproduces all 184 realized guarded returns and breaches by resolving only ties in the highest-vote group. This led to the separate within-tier attribution and matched early-stopping study. [Fresh review](robustness-r9-fresh.md) · [Foldwise decomposition](../evidence/ordinal_decomposition/folds.csv) |
| 10 | Continuous minimum scores also have a valid early-stopping bound. The earlier time comparison short-circuited only the integer vote. A numerical check found that crossing a strict vote tier changed a Parkinson model's AUC despite equal effective compression. | The new implementation gives every running minimum its own exact bound and gives all methods a fresh score cache. Each bounded rule recovered its exhaustive nominee on every one of 184 slates. The paper gives the two actual Parkinson AUCs and reports costs separately from predictive outcomes. [Matched stopping tests](../../tests/test_matched_stopping.py) · [Independent cost audit](robustness-r10-cost-review.md) |
