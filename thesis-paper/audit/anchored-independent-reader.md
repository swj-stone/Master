# Independent manuscript review

## Five-page version, 17 November 2026

I read and rendered all five pages of the anonymous PDF with SHA-256 `12a16765a23d39d8df0b79be1bf5571b34bdade549ee0f1dabc5909324df8d93`. I formed the following judgment from the manuscript, the study tables and an independent calculation of the example on pages 2–3. I did not consult other reviews.

**Editorial judgment.** The five prospective tasks support a short empirical methods paper on selecting XGBoost inputs with a small refit budget. The most informative finding is what happens when the simpler comparator receives the same search budget. I would decline a general-ML main-track submission claiming a predictive or computational advantage for the minimum vote. I would consider a specialist workshop or empirical short-paper submission after the comparisons below are made visible. This judges the contribution and its evidence, independently of a particular venue's formatting rules.

1. **Show the closest controls directly.** Table 1 gives all its space to exhaustive Gain. Against Gain with identical anchor and refinement opportunities, MinPI took 1.044, 1.140, 1.018, 1.094 and 1.199 times as long in median paired time on Vowel, HCV, Shopping, Garment and Tetouan respectively. The paired median primary-score changes were zero on the first four and +0.00134 R² on Tetouan, where widths were six versus nine. These are the central comparisons for deciding whether to pay for voting. Give paired time, width and score alongside each other. Min and mean selected identical sets and produced identical test predictions in every prospective pair. Those data provide no evidence of a benefit from taking the minimum.

2. **Identify what the 25 pairs repeat.** The seed table records exactly one distinct prediction hash and one returned support per method across all five seeds for each of Shopping, Garment and Tetouan under both MinPI and exhaustive Gain. The same holds for the matched Gain control. Thus five of nine changed supports versus matched Gain repeat a single Tetouan predictive contrast. On Vowel, fit and validation speakers move but all fits predict the same seven held-out speakers. The source tables reproduce the 25/25 speed-and-width successes versus exhaustive Gain and the 23/25 successes after the quality threshold. Ten of those 25 runs use regression R², and both predictive misses occur on Vowel. Say this explicitly in the abstract. The held-speaker AUC interval for Vowel is [−0.0168, +0.0120] and the held-day R² interval for Garment is [−0.0614, +0.0449]. Both extend below the chosen −0.01 tolerance. Shopping has one held-out month. Report these as conditional uncertainty where supported and preserve the descriptive interpretation of success counts.

3. **Keep the theory's scope precise.** Equation (2) follows by excluding the other representatives from the size-`k` draw. In the example the worst shares of A and B are 10/47 and 11/50 and the average reverses their ordering. The validation guard is a direct invariant for any ranking. It gives up to six search fits including the full fit, plus one deployment fit. These are sound modest results. Clarify the fallback when every tested size has a zero denominator. With seven interchangeable columns and positive shared weight, every coalition of sizes 1, 3 and 5 already covers the voter. Positive uncovered weight can remain before the greedy step while all three marginal totals are zero. State that the specified Gain tie order completes the ranking in that case.

4. **Keep the timing questions distinct.** The prospective CPU comparison charges fitting through final prediction, and the frozen-versus-packed check holds the supports and predictions fixed. On the 76-column Bankruptcy anchor the 92.14 to 18.09 ms figures are whole-ranking measurements. Its median paired complete-pipeline saving was about 44 ms and packed MinPI took about 137 ms longer than matched Gain. The retrospective RFECV and SelectFromModel comparisons also reproduce from their separate source table. With the prescribed single validation split, RFECV's median paired time ratios range from 0.390 to 0.570 for MinPI, but its Garment R² exceeds MinPI's by 0.0247. The threshold selector was faster on all five. These observations identify the comparator, split and cost that a claim can cover.

**Original-six extension.** For the later GPU screen, label the datasets' prior use, input representation and full dimensions. Identify the exact comparator, common splits, refits and timing window, including shared or separately charged preparation, synchronization and prediction. Show per-task retained width, elapsed time and classification AUC or regression R² with the companion accuracy or MAE where available. Report actual run and seed counts and adverse results. Keep CPU and GPU comparisons within their measured regimes. Only a matched minimum-versus-mean comparison can identify a distinct empirical effect of the minimum. A completed GPU execution by itself changes neither that attribution nor the independence of the original data.
