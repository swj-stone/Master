The unit of review was a saved PDF. The design reader wrote assessment criteria from the earlier manuscript before seeing the new analyses. The statistical reader and the manuscript reader worked from separate instructions, without consulting each other's reviews. Each could inspect code and independently recompute the evidence. The author received criticisms after the readings. These are internal AI-assisted reviews. An external program committee has not evaluated these artifacts.
