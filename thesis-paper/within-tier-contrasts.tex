\paragraph{Uncertainty for choice within a tied group.}
An analyst who nominates uniformly draws once among the maximum-vote candidates before the guard. We computed the expected guarded return by averaging each nominee's own outcome, including its possible fallback. Every rule used that same eligible group. The point and full rules give any remaining exact tie to the first candidate in frozen slate order. A nonactionable fold returns the anchor under every rule.
