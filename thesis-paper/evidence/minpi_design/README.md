# Development decision on refitted minimum voting

The six original prepared datasets and seven previously studied application datasets were development material. The four new replication tasks remained sealed. Every reported fold uses seed 42. Native development timings used CPU hist with one thread while research jobs used other cores. Treat small timing differences as descriptive. The shared full fit and each cached native check are charged at their complete measured time to every arm that uses them. Reading identical dataset files and computing final held metrics are excluded from these timings.

## Compensated votes

Let G be the ridge-penalized fitting Gram matrix after whitening each feature block. Its diagonal blocks are identities. Let g be the feature–response cross moment. Subtract the empty predictor's loss, divide by the marginal chance of observing a feature, and retain the same ridge penalty. For a uniform mask of size s out of p > 1 inputs, write t = (s − 1)/(p − 1). Conditional on one feature being observed, another distinct feature is observed with probability t. The normalized expected loss therefore equals Lₜ(b) = −2⟨g,b⟩ + ⟨b, ((1 − t)I + tG)b⟩. This game uses continuous performance votes and changes the older ternary vote definition. Marginalization under feature corruption has prior work. [van der Maaten et al., ICML 2013](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v28/vandermaaten13.html)

The vote at a mask size is minus regret relative to the best full fit for that same size. For a fixed head, regret Lₜ(b) − minᵤ Lₜ(u) is convex in t because the pointwise infimum of affine functions is concave. Its worst value over all sizes occurs at either endpoint. On each support we fit a common head to minimize the larger endpoint regret. Epigraph duality for two convex quadratics gives a scalar endpoint mixture. Fixing the mixture at its current optimum gives a lower bound for each possible deletion's reoptimized worst regret. Let Vⱼ be the diagonal block for feature j of the inverse regularized normal matrix H⁻¹, where H = (1 − t)I + tG. Constrained quadratic minimization gives the increase at that mixture as trace(bⱼᵀ Vⱼ⁻¹ bⱼ). Visit deletions by increasing bound and stop once the bound exceeds the best rescored objective. The greedy decision agrees with exhaustive rescoring up to the declared numerical tolerance.

Direct mask enumeration, an independent epigraph solution and screened-versus-exhaustive rankings under ties are checked in `test_game.py`. On 13 development sources the linear basis required 634 exact candidate solves out of 17,777 possible and the three-term spline basis required 592. Reading `prepared-pilot.csv` and `seven-pilot.csv` gives each task, head, ranker, held metrics and selection-only time. The surrogate head can perform poorly on the underlying prediction task. For example, the compensated two-column linear Madelon classifier achieved held AUC 0.615. Pruning quadratic solves does not establish that the complete selection pipeline is faster than native Gain.

## Native refit

The first native check compares subset proposals from raw linear features against ordinary Gain using identical XGBoost receiver models. A linear proxy-width gate frequently skipped useful smaller widths. On Spambase its minimum-vote gate retained all 56 inputs, while the ascending minimum-vote path used 32 and ascending Gain used 16. See `native-six-pilot.csv`.

The revision used the full native booster's approximate additive path contributions for up to 512 fitting records. Full inputs were available while constructing the game. Every deployed predictor was a true refit on the selected raw features. The surrogate has no exact equivalence to a native reduced-feature refit. Plain mean absolute contributions and ordinary Gain were included as practical controls. The XGBoost path contribution calculation is documented by its authors. [XGBoost prediction documentation](https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/prediction.html)

With identical dyadic bisection, minimum voting matched Gain's retained width on five of six sources and reduced ISOLET from 100 to 64. Credit Card Fraud AUC was 0.9749 versus 0.9416 at eight features for both methods. The joint criterion requires equal or smaller width, lower charged time and both held score tolerances from the protocol. Minimum voting met this criterion on zero of six sources against bisected Gain, three against ascending Gain, one against bisected pooled deletion and zero against bisected mean absolute contributions. Its median time ratio to bisected Gain was 1.176. Plain magnitude retained four inputs on Abalone, sixteen on Gisette and two on Credit Card Fraud, compared to eight, thirty-two and eight with minimum voting. Bisection can skip qualifying widths when validation scores are nonmonotonic. These results do not support promotion to the four fresh sources.

All native outcomes appear in `path-six-pilot.csv`, with paired decisions in `path-paired.csv`. For provenance `archived-reference.csv` gives the older exhaustive Gain values on the prepared datasets. The archive used CUDA, all widths and a different training/selection policy. Its wall times cannot measure the incremental benefit of minimum voting. Use the same-device, same-budget controls for that comparison.
