# Bounded restoration and exchange on nineteen opened sources

A support can be immune to every single deletion and equal-width improving swap yet admit one restored feature and two deletions. Four additive columns proportional to (0.8, 1.2, 2, −2) along a unit margin direction give an example. The first two alone reproduce the full teacher. At a 20% relative radius, neither single deletion nor any equal-width swap is feasible. Returning the third column allows the original two to be removed with zero distortion. The independent brute-force checks verify this example and matched direct/voted local moves on random overlapping path games.

The bounded search evaluates up to eight restorations, four first deletions per restoration, every remaining second deletion, and up to four accepted moves, with ordinary feasible deletion after each. In a group-truncated forest, returning a column reactivates exactly the paths for which it was the only missing variable. The directional control uses three coalition-size expected teacher directions to veto candidates before measuring their full norm. Its direct counterpart uses identical neighborhoods and partial-distance elimination.

All 95 paired splits from the six historical and thirteen already-open sources were evaluated. The repaired exact greedy start retained 0.247% fewer columns than its original support (ratio .9975, source bootstrap .9950–.9995). Relative to gain-order sweep, the same rescue retained 11.27% fewer. Relative to static singleton-ablation sweep it retained 1.58% fewer. Once both starts receive rescue, exact and static widths have ratio .9996. The signed checks never changed a support and directed exact repair cost about 0.89% more than direct repair in full pipeline time. These data support correcting weaker starting supports. They do not support the intended 5–10% additional saving over the strongest starts. The reported endpoints are computationally bounded local solutions.

The study protocol and executable geometry checks are `experiments/minpi_rework/quota_compress_rescue.md` and `quota_compress_rescue_check.py`. `scores-all.csv` and `contrasts-all.csv` retain the five-seed results and controls.
