# Independent quantitative audit of predictive MinPI

All 35 frozen splits and 1,295 source–seed–arm outputs were accounted for. A separate verifier reproduced the intact predictions by summing archived visible-path increments for each retained support. It recalculated empirical macro AUC by explicitly comparing positive/negative scores, together with accuracy, R², MAE and scaled MAE. Maximum absolute errors were 1.82 × 10⁻¹² for individual predictions and 5.69 × 10⁻¹⁴ for the reported metrics. On each of the seven seed-42 tests it also enumerated every possible availability mask for every archived support, with up to 65,536 masks per support. The largest error among the reported worst, mean, intact and positive-excess risks was 1.61 × 10⁻¹⁵. This checks the conditional-loss calculations against the sealed path increments. It provides no population-risk certificate. [Audit rows](independent-mask-audit.csv) · [Verifier](../../../../experiments/minpi_rework/predictive_mask_audit.py)

The test concerns seven applications with 10–16 original columns. Five seeds share the same chronological test on each of Beijing and Seoul. Development labels independently matched the raw UCI outcomes and forecast horizons. No partition split a literal input-vector duplicate or a declared group. **Practical primary.** The candidate family containing MDI can always match MDI's accepted validation width. The registered goal was a further 10% saving. MDI + min removed 0.429 additional columns on average (3.95% of the original width). Its paired geometric retained-width ratio to MDI was 0.935 [0.864, 1.000]. All seven smaller supports occurred in Beijing (4/5) or Indian liver (3/5). The other 28 tied. Its total time ratio was 1.247 [1.146, 1.356], based on the registered accounting that reuses shared forest computations. Relative to MDI, its mean clean differences were −0.0010 AUC and −0.0009 accuracy on classification, −0.0009 R² and +0.0022 scaled MAE on regression. Outer quota breaches increased from 9 to 10 of 35. Relative to the full forest, the three-source regression means crossed both clean-loss limits (−0.0358 R², +0.0241 scaled MAE). Seoul contributed −0.086 R² and +0.046 scaled MAE. On Indian liver, mean AUC fell by 0.014 vs full. The primary width target failed.

**Contribution of the ranking.** Under matched two-path search and the identical minimum guard, the pooled-ranking alternative used fewer columns than minimum ranking on four splits, more on one and the same on 30. The geometric min/pooled width ratio was 1.009 [0.990, 1.031]. Both breached 10/35 times. The min/MDI-plus-static width ratio was 0.948 [0.884, 1.007] and accounted time ratio 1.068 [1.033, 1.105]. The comparison supports no claim that minimum ranking improves on the strongest matched candidate family.

**Contribution of acceptance.** On the same MDI + minimum-ranking paths, changing pooled to minimum acceptance reduced mean held-out positive worst-size excess loss from 0.02630 to 0.02222. The difference was −0.00408 [−0.0081, −0.0008]. Four splits moved from breaching to passing and none moved the other way. Breach rates were 14/35 and 10/35 (difference −11.4 percentage points [−22.9, −2.9]). Mean relative width increased by a geometric factor of 1.043 [1.011, 1.082]. Four sources contributed zero change in the breach rate. The exact two-sided sign test on the three nonzero source differences gives 0.25. The seven-source percentile interval describes this panel; it cannot support reliable protection on a new application. All five Seoul seeds breached under both rules.
