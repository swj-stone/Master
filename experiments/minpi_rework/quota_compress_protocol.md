# Directional-vote compression of a fitted additive predictor

Use a linear additive teacher, fit once on the pooled original fit and vote partitions. On the disjoint width partition center its feature contributions at their training means. Set the allowed Euclidean score discrepancy to 0.20 times the norm of the centered full teacher score, jointly across rows and outputs. Each support is deployed by trimming that fitted teacher. All arms receive exactly this calibration budget. The main calibration outcome is number of retained original inputs. The outer metrics are paired AUC and accuracy or R² and MAE. Original prepared tasks also report the previously published novel-input sensitivity. Selection, votes and budgets cannot see outer outcomes.

Use seed 42 for the six original prepared sources and eight previously opened UCI sources (Glass, Raisin, WiFi, Leaves, Airfoil, Yacht, Slump, Energy). Evaluate (a) the first feasible energy-ranked and direct XGBoost Gain prefixes, (b) a backward deletion/one-swap local search that directly measures fidelity, (c) a cardinality-minimizing linear ballot master with and without explicit conditional variable fixing, and (d) a native convex quadratic constraint solver with the same local feasible incumbent. Source selection depends only on the archived panels. No fresh test source enters this study.

Every residual direction gives a necessary signed halfspace of the fidelity ball. At a trial support, its own nonzero residual separates any infeasible support. Initial directions are the full target, each feature and its negative, and intermediate residuals of the backward search. Use only the necessary condition that a width-K completion can add its K largest positive free weights. A ballot failing this bound under inclusion certifies exclusion; failing under exclusion certifies inclusion. Iterate certified fixings to a fixed point. Report these at the feasible incumbent budget, preserving all qualifying supports of at most that size. Report global lower bounds, best verified feasible width, optimal status, signed-group counts, solver cost and outer quality separately. Both integer-master variants keep the same initial ballots, incumbent, stopping criterion and constraint-generation policy. The native solver uses the exact conic fidelity constraint. Limit each optimization arm to ten solver/wall seconds, allow at most the final solver shutdown overhead, and keep all timed-out incumbents. An exact calibration optimum certifies only the specified finite teacher-fidelity problem.
