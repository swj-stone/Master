# Specialized quadratic branch-and-bound control

This secondary comparison was specified after the first four sources of the SCIP run and before implementing or timing this control. Repeat the same eight sources, regional games, seed and three timing repetitions. Replace exhaustive enumeration by a compiled cardinality-constrained quadratic branch-and-bound. All arms receive the same one-swap regional incumbent as native SCIP. Screened arms also receive the fixed variables and residual order. Refit the teacher in each timed run, exclude source loading and JIT compilation, record time, visited nodes, prunes, objective and support. Compare both total and search time to raw branch-and-bound and the prior unrestricted-order screen. Include the raw specialized solver even if its speed removes a previously observed advantage.

For a node with selected A, remaining R and t slots, let l_i denote the linear contribution of i after interactions with A. Write pair coefficient a_ij for the utility increment when both i and j are present. For each available i form an upper bound b_i by adding to l_i half the sum of the t−1 largest a_ij among the other available items. For every t-set T, summing these bounds over i in T covers the value of its internal pairs, including negative coefficients. The sum of the t largest b_i therefore gives an admissible completion bound. Precompute the per-feature neighbor terms for each suffix and t in a fixed order. A residual strict precedence order may remove further completions. Use conservative numerical margins before pruning and verify returned utility against direct enumeration. This is an independent objective upper bound. It makes no single-feature dominance comparisons.
