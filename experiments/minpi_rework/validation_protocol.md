# Fresh panel for MinPI compression

## Estimand and decision

The original six prepared tasks and five seeds measure continuity with PIv0/PIv1. Their labels and earlier results have informed method design. Develop on these or other previously opened sources. The eight sources below evaluate transfer to specified numeric tasks with at most 100 inputs. They are a purposive sample, containing one simulator. Source selection and preprocessing were fixed before inspecting any outer model score. HTRU2, Seeds, Vertebral Column and Vehicle had appeared in this project's earlier experiments and were excluded during the source inventory. Neither rankings nor inner scores from the fresh panel may be used to choose the entrant.

Before fitting the fresh panel, record the exact candidate, prediction learner(s), matched ungated/pooled comparator, mature comparator(s), implementation/configuration hashes, primary width or time contrast and intended domain. If these differ by receiver, name the scoped claims. Use an inexpensive, competitive comparator for the same learner, as well as direct Gain on the original data. For example, a linear receiver requires a cheap coefficient path; a nonlinear receiver requires at least one competitive nonlinear ranking. An incremental claim for the minimum needs a same-data, same-budget voting ablation (pooled reduction is suitable). For any competing shortcut, reproduce identical ties, width proposals, inner floors and receiver for the strict matched comparison. Report the cost each arm would incur when run by itself. An extra cost imposed only on a comparator invalidates a speed claim.

For each source, average five paired differences in held-out predictive scores. Retain native AUC and accuracy or native R² and MAE. For domain summaries also divide MAE by the standard deviation of the development targets from the corresponding split. A measured predictive difference falls within the retained practical margins when both AUC and accuracy decrease by at most .01, or R² decreases by at most .02 and scaled MAE increases by at most .02. Compare with both the full receiver and the named compressed comparator. Empirical margins describe these sources. Population noninferiority would require its own sampling design and precision.

Compute paired width ratios and time ratios per run on the log scale. Average over seeds within source, then equally over sources in each prediction domain. Conditional on acceptable predictive differences, call a practical improvement a width ratio at most .90 or an end-to-end time ratio at most .90 with width ratio at most 1. End-to-end time includes preprocessing, ranking, validation fits and final training/prediction; report selection-only time alongside. These numerical targets are project operating goals from the prior study. Do not silently switch between methods, baselines, statistical units or domains after scoring. Report source results and adverse exceptions even when an average passes. Show the no-minimum and no-vote controls and state the size, direction, cost and uncertainty of the difference they isolate. A fast prefix routine shared by all arms can support an implementation result. Retaining voting in the name cannot establish an incremental benefit from voting. Count the sources on which the practical criterion holds and disclose sign inconsistencies. For uncertainty resample entire paired sources after averaging seeds and calculate descriptive 95% percentile intervals. Four sources per domain give coarse intervals.

Use the same processors, threads, deployment budget and independently complete timing for each arm, with rotated order when repeating clocks. Identify GPU/CPU and the exact number of real fits and validation predictions. If machine load compromises a clock comparison, repeat just the frozen timing with predictions/support verified; outputs remain fixed. Keep timeout, missing and failed jobs in the denominator and distinguish them from completed rows. A single dataset/seed over one hour requires intervention. A source-specific exception or negative result stays in the record.

An older frozen study illustrates when to redesign a policy before using new holdouts. Even crediting every legal proposed deletion, the one-proposal rule could save at most 8.44% in classification and 12.67% in regression over the base coefficient path. Five of fifteen classification splits had an opportunity, all on Musk. Actual improvements were 3.55% and 5.93%, respectively, while selection time multiplied by 1.31 and 1.64. Both equal-size controls retained at most as many features. Use development-only proposal opportunities and cost envelopes to eliminate similar infeasible policies. A failed fresh panel becomes development evidence for future revisions; repeated evaluation on it cannot confirm subsequent adaptations. [Prior bound and observed results](../../thesis-paper/evidence/minpi_compression/witness-transfer/README.md)

## Data and prediction question

All files are original UCI-hosted inputs under CC BY 4.0, with independent byte digests in [the manifest](validation_sources.json). The following table credits the sources and describes the derived analysis views.

| Source | Rows × inputs | Predictors, outcome, partition unit |
|---|---:|---|
| [Colonna et al., Anuran Calls](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC9H) | 7,195 × 22 | MFCCs only, Leptodactylidae (23 recordings) versus the three remaining families (37 recordings). Two families have only five recordings each, so one-versus-rest reduces sparse strata in the four-way partition. All syllables from a recording (one frog) stay together. |
| [Higuera et al., Mice Protein Expression](https://doi.org/10.24432/C50S3Z) | 1,080 × 77 | Genotype Ts65Dn versus control from protein concentrations, excluding treatment, behavior, combined class and identifiers. Keep each mouse's 15 technical readings together. Fit median imputation on training rows. |
| [Antal and Hajdu, Diabetic Retinopathy Debrecen](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5XP4P) | 1,151 × 18 | Image descriptors, including lesion counts at multiple thresholds. Exclude the precomputed AM/FM classifier output (column 18, zero-based). Predict presence of diabetic retinopathy. Patient IDs are unavailable; exact descriptor duplicates stay together. This evaluates image-level discrimination. |
| [Patrício et al., Breast Cancer Coimbra](https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P59) | 116 × 9 | Quantitative anthropometric and blood-test measurements, patient versus healthy control. All nine inputs retained, including HOMA. Small-sample, low-dimensional stress case; a single outer error changes accuracy by about four percentage points. |
| [Candanedo et al., Appliances Energy Prediction](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G) | 3,290 × 26 | Predict appliance Wh during the ten-minute reading at the top of each hour from contemporaneous environmental descriptors and the two supplied random controls. Exclude simultaneous light-meter usage and timestamp. Hold out complete days in increasing time order. This is same-building temporal transport. |
| [Hamidieh, Superconductivty Data](https://doi.org/10.24432/C53P47) | 4,000 × 81 | Predict critical temperature in kelvin from derived chemistry features. Join rows by material formula or identical predictor vectors before sampling and splitting. Pair the two original files by their published row alignment, checking all critical temperatures. |
| [Arzamasov, Electrical Grid Stability](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5PG66) | 4,000 × 12 | Simulated grid response (continuous stability eigenvalue); exclude its binary sign. The producer power is a deterministic sum of consumer powers. Group identical inputs. This is a known redundant, simulated control. |
| [Zhou, Geographical Origin of Music](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VK5D) | 1,059 × 68 | First public feature view (68 timbral descriptors), target latitude in degrees. Longitude and latitude never enter X. Hold out entire distinct capital coordinates (33 origins). The publisher standardized these descriptors on the public collection; refit standardization within training partitions. Artist IDs are unavailable. |

To bound training cost on Superconductivity and Grid, randomly permute whole groups with seed 71117 and take the longest leading prefix containing at most 4,000 rows. The cap and RNG are independent of all method outputs and outcomes. All other sources retain their declared rows. Keep exact predictor duplicates in the same split, including transitive links to subject/material IDs. Each of the five seeds (42–46) partitions groups into fit, vote, width and outer sets with approximately 48/16/16/20% of the groups; for classification stratify groups by the class of the first source row in each group. Appliances uses one chronological split by days across all five seeds. Repeat seeds in that task vary the selector only. Default metrics weight instances within each source. For recordings and mouse assays, report subject-balanced sensitivity for a subject-level interpretation. Record the level and weights whenever changing metrics.

For diagnosis, four synthetic controls share 1,600 observations and 48 standardized-in-population dimensions. In the dense pair each column carries the same absolute signal; in the proxy pair each of eight latent factors has six noisy measurements. Matched regression and classification use the same latent noisy response, with the latter thresholded at zero. Biological or deployment conclusions require the public-source results. The synthetic tasks remain a separate sensitivity table.

The pre-score integrity check verified disjoint group identities between every pair of partitions in all 40 public-source splits, no conflicting binary targets within the constructed groups, and the outer-label sentinel on every outer row. This check is limited to released metadata and exact duplicate vectors. It cannot identify unrecorded participants, near-duplicates or laboratory/site effects.

## Running and locking

Run `.\thesis-python.ps1 -m experiments.minpi_rework.validation_panel --fetch` to retrieve/check the pinned files and verify all partitions. `validation_panel.split(name, seed)` returns `X, y, task, idx` with sentinel outer labels. Fit all imputation, standardization, voting and supervised filters freshly on their allowed partitions. Seal all per-arm outer predictions and selected supports, the expected source/seed/arm list, configuration and code hashes in one committed manifest prior to evaluating any fresh outcome. `validation_panel.score(name, seed, path, frozen_sha256)` checks the digest and returns metrics for every array in a locked npz; the driver must verify full panel coverage and arm identity before calling it. No deployment inference or clinical action is implied by this benchmark.
