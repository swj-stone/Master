# Beijing air monitor outages, seed 42

This secondary, single-archive diagnostic is specified after the seven-source study. The earlier Beijing experiment used complete records from one of these stations. Its scores were already known. This protocol is recorded before fitting or scoring the new 12-station cohort.

Use the hash-pinned UCI Beijing Multi-Site Air-Quality Data archive linked by the [primary source manifest](validation_predictive_sources.json). Include every station and every noon origin with an observed PM2.5 measurement at noon the following day at the same station. The ten numeric predictor fields and two wind-direction coordinates match the earlier Beijing task. Preserve all missing origin readings. No station name, contemporaneous PM2.5, time stamp or future feature enters the model. Treat wind sine and cosine as two unavailable columns when direction is absent. Report missing targets and row counts. Calculations weight each eligible station-day equally.

Divide the original sequence of forecast-origin dates at 48%, 64% and 80%, before filtering on target availability. All stations share these cuts. Exclude every origin whose next-day response falls in the following block. Train one 100-tree depth-four random forest with seed 42 and training-only median imputation. Use the next two blocks for ranking and width acceptance, and the final block for scoring. No resampling or selection over seed, station, width quota or time window. Document that outcome observation is a condition for inclusion.

Compare two ways to form the size votes. The ordinary method uses the fit medians for absent measurements. The observation-aware method zeroes each row's tree-path increment whenever a column required by that increment is actually absent. It then applies the same exact vote over a further uniform size-s subset of the twelve original columns. The intersection with the row's observed columns is used by both the full and compressed forests. This law is conditional on the recorded outage pattern. It does not describe the process that caused the outages. In each design rank by MDI alone and by the MDI + greedy-MinPI portfolio. Apply the same .02 worst-size acceptance quota and native R²/scaled-MAE tolerances to both. The latter checks use median or stopped predictions consistent with the corresponding design. A further common-guard comparison reranks only the ordinary MDI + MinPI paths under the observation-aware acceptance rule. Compare all selected supports and the full forest under both median-imputed and stopped inference on the same outer observations.

Report width, predictive mean, MSE, MAE and R² for all eligible outer station-days, those with an incomplete origin and those with a complete origin. Give counts of represented stations, dates and missing predictors. Report paired changes in MSE and MAE against the matching full-support inference and full-support stopped versus median. Descriptive 95% intervals resample full synchronized seven-day blocks of origin dates (circular moving-block bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 71201), preserving stations within dates. Report the number of usable resamples. The station network is one dependent source. No additional hyperparameter choices or source-level generalization tests follow these outcomes.

Before opening outer metrics, save the support and keyed forecasts and record their digests. Check the observation-aware path expansion against direct feature-restricted tree traversal, including completely unavailable rows, and for a small fixed subset validate the analytic size risks against direct mask enumeration. Support overlap, lack of improvement and unstable subgroup outcomes are reported.
