# Prospective comparison of analytic and sampled predictive-risk computation

This computational endpoint is registered before any forecast or outcome evaluation for the seven new sources in the predictive confirmation panel. It uses the subsequently frozen seven-source manifest as a whole, the declared grouping rules and seeds 42–46. It is separate from selection of a portfolio on the acceptance sample. The fitting sample alone trains the shared sklearn probability/regression forest (100 rounds of tree fitting, maximum depth four, one CPU thread, training median imputation). The same forest and voting sample define all calibration risk games. No acceptance or test labels enter computation, timing, approximation checks or the stopping rule.

For the greedy search with the .02 normalized predictive excess-loss quota, compare the following implementations. The exact reference is the risk from the hypergeometric inclusion moment in every positive coalition size. It uses the native production calculation and a separately constructed dictionary that reduces linear and quadratic terms with the same required union to one coefficient. A compiled exact recount reconstructs all candidate increments from active terms at every step. The analytic incremental and sampled incremental alternatives retire each affected term once using the same update engine. The latter computes empirical first-inclusion distributions from a shared bank of uniformly random permutations and their reversals. Eight, 32 and 128 count both orientations. The three budgets use nested prefix banks. We use one reproducible independent bank per source/seed and preserve common random numbers for competing deletions. At comparable implementations allow sparse first-inclusion histograms or the optimized dense histograms and disclose both if reported as an envelope.

Record one warm-up and three interleaved timed repeats per computational arm (median within job), including source-specific moment setup, dictionary construction where required, search and shared training/trace times. Exclude input file loading, JIT compilation and correctness checks from the selector clock and give their scope separately. Do not charge the union dictionary to implementations which do not need it. For the source-equal worst-case time comparison between analytic and empirical weights, prespecify 128 permutations, with 8 and 32 secondary. The estimand is the paired geometric selector wall-time ratio. Report equally weighted source means of paired log ratios across seeds with source-resampling percentile intervals, individual source exceptions, and training-inclusive costs.

Independently run native mask-prediction Monte Carlo for seed 42 without constructing the Gram or union dictionary when its storage requirement is at most 512 MiB and conservative work index B × n_output_coordinates × p² × (p+1) / 2 is at most 6×10^9 at the particular budget. Both quantities depend only on calibration dimensions and B. List all excluded source/budget cells and the reasons. Use the same common permutation bank, quota and tie policy. Also compare direct masked curves for the selected support at 8 permutations on all feasible sources. A direct per-support check does not count as a complete search time.

Independently check complete exact paths, sampled-versus-direct paths where measured, Jaccard similarity, support/sequence equality, calibration violations under the exact risk, and maximum error across normalized size risks at both the fixed exact support and the adaptively selected sampled support. Report zeros and failures. Outcome-side comparisons, if added, require locked forecasts for all involved arms under the seven-source release protocol before any scoring. These computations can establish exact empirical integration and measured computational costs. They do not establish an advantage in prediction from worst-size voting or coverage for natural sensor failures.
