# Prospective third-round source panel

These five sources were chosen independently of selector outcomes and frozen before any fit. Eligibility required real measurements from a collection absent from earlier project experiments, at least five numerical inputs, a distinct target, a documented unit of observation, and readily distributable public files. Choose one source each for speech, blood assays, web sessions, industrial labour and electricity demand. This is a purposive narrow-panel stress test of exhaustive Gain. Dataset counts are five, including failures. Four of five must satisfy the prespecified joint gate. The two prior panels remain reported separately. No dataset or seed is replaced. See the parent third-round algorithm protocol for the frozen selector, comparators and score tolerances.

Candidate checking covered paths and alias searches in the project configs, experiments, thesis evidence, archived history and local raw-data inventory. Cited exclusions include Urban Land Cover, Dry Bean, Rice (Cammeo/Osmancik), ozone, optical digits, hand-written pen digits, Steel Plates, wine quality, Musk, Landsat, Wall-Following, Seeds and Vertebral Column, which had appeared in earlier work. Individual household power shares target-derived aggregate components. The Egyptian fertility and small breast tissue candidates have very small rare classes. No scores, feature rankings or fitted models were used to screen sources. Deterding vowel speakers and utterances differ from the original isolated-letter ISOLET collection. The Tetouan utility is distinct from Turkish power-plant and Seoul meteorological records. Garment observations are team production records, distinct from earlier student or wearable activity studies.

| Source and prediction task | Inputs, partitions and limits |
|---|---|
| [Deterding et al., UCI 152](https://doi.org/10.24432/C58P4S). Eleven steady-state British English vowels. | Ten LPC-derived acoustic inputs. Use the official seven outer test speakers. Shuffle the eight distinct training speakers by seed, using four for fit and two each for vote and width. Omit split flag, speaker ID and sex. Repeated seeds reuse the same 462 outer utterances. |
| [Lichtinghagen et al., UCI 571](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5D612). Published donor versus confirmed hepatitis, fibrosis or cirrhosis category. | Omit the ambiguous suspect donor category, patient serial identifier and sex. Use age and ten laboratory measurements with native missingness. Shuffle groups of identical model-input profiles by seed with proportions .48/.16/.16/.20. This is retrospective donor–patient discrimination, not screening accuracy for an unselected clinical population. |
| [Sakar and Kastro, UCI 468](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5F88Q). Revenue at the end of a session. | Publisher reports a distinct user per session. Use six session page counts/durations plus SpecialDay and Weekend. Omit PageValues, which uses transactions, and site-aggregate BounceRates/ExitRates, whose future-data availability is unknown. Select up to 8,192 original rows by a fixed independent uniform draw, seed 617. Fit through September, vote in October, width in November, test in December. Interpret as end-of-session prediction at the same site. |
| [Imran et al., UCI 597](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/597/productivity). Team-shift actual productivity. | Use day of week, day of month, department, planned productivity, standard minute value, style changes and staff count. Exclude team IDs, incentive, overtime, unfinished output and idle counters. Hold whole work dates apart chronologically at group fractions .48/.16/.16/.20. Teams may repeat across dates. This measures new dates in the same factory, conditional on the given shift descriptors. |
| [Salam and El Hibaoui, UCI 849](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5B034). Zone 1 electrical demand. | Keep minute-zero hourly readings, five meteorological measurements and sine/cosine pairs for local hour and day of week. Exclude all three electricity outputs from the inputs. Hold whole days apart in chronological order with fractions .48/.16/.16/.20. This predicts contemporaneous demand given measured weather and time. |

Seeds are 42–46. Only HCV has a resampled outer partition. Vowel changes inner speaker partitions only. The three time panels share the same splits across seeds. Treat the collection, not the seed, as the independent comparison unit. Group identifiers and known predictors cross-panel similarities do not enter the fitted learner. Native XGBoost handles missing values. Lock all 25 source–seed experiments and every arm's support, input hashes and predictions before using the provided truth function.
