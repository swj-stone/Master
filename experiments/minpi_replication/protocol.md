## Common efficacy target

Use the complete, frozen, same-learner direct gain sparse-selection recipe with all operational work charged. For each source compare candidate to direct gain, paired within seed on identical partitions and preprocessing. Average the operative wall times, selected widths and outer metrics over its five seeds. A source succeeds jointly if the candidate takes strictly less end-to-end wall time, uses at most the control width plus the larger of one column and 5% of the control width, and loses at most .01 of macro one-vs-rest AUC (classification) or R² (regression). The target is at least 80% of the four sources, hence all four. This working tolerance was specified at the lead researcher's request before scoring. Also report accuracy and raw MAE, and MAE normalized by fitting-sample outcome SD to keep units comparable. Report full-input score, actual width/speed and outcomes of minimum vs pooled on the identical candidate generator, fit budget, solver, guard and timing boundary. A candidate beating a weak baseline while losing to pooled cannot establish the incremental value of the minimum. Use repeated warmed, interleaved timing or report any deviation. No source or seed may be discarded following unfavorable results.

The subject and temporal groups, controls and marginal tolerances govern selection independent of test results. Fit imputation, scaling and any learned transforms strictly inside the fitting partition. If an algorithm needs additional inner partitions they must be drawn from labeled development rows; test outcomes remain masked. Do not give an algorithm access to the original future targets via fitting arrays. Save source/seed/method, input support and forecasts hash before releasing any outer scores. Classification outputs require ordered probabilities or decision scores for all classes; undefined metrics are recorded explicitly.
