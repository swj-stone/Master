# Sensitivity to the candidate draw

The local ANOVA experiment selects the F prefix and up to seven one-column exchanges. The sensitivity analysis repeats only the exchange draw, using seeds 87201, 87202 and 87203. Sources, split assignments, forests and their seeds, background, anchor, guard and return criteria stay fixed. The two additional draws were specified at `d135990` after sealing the first draw, and their nominations and forecasts were sealed at `2488b7f` before scoring. For a fixed fold and support, the same guard and outer refits apply to every draw that contains it. The 266 initial candidate records and 299 additional distinct fold/support records represent 565 candidate records across 46 folds. The analysis averages the three draws within each fold, then the folds within each source, and finally the sources equally. The paired 30,000-draw percentile intervals resample sources and preserve all their fold and draw outcomes. The guarded breach rate increased by 2.22 points [0.00, 6.67] on the first panel and by 1.85 [0.00, 5.56] on the wider panel under the full margin relative to the vote.

On the three-draw average, effective compression for F prefix, uniform, vote, full margin and point margin was 15.99%, 14.20%, 18.65%, 16.43% and 17.51% respectively on the first panel. On the wider panel it was 11.11%, 11.32%, 11.57%, 12.89% and 12.00%. Urban's full-minus-vote difference was +16.22 points. Parkinson and Semeion contributed −2.78 and −5.56 points. All other wider sources tied. The direction of the wider panel's realized difference changes with the candidate draw. For each fold we computed the Jaccard index of its candidate sets for the three pairs of draws, including the common F prefix and excluding the anchor. The mean across all fold/pair combinations was .775 on the ten-task panel and .071 on the wider panel. Pools were identical on 63 of 90 first-panel and zero of 48 wider-panel combinations. Source counts and available comparison sets therefore constrain what these three draws can say about unseen pools. This calculation describes three fixed draws on the same sources and outer observations. [Overlap counts](../../thesis-paper/evidence/neighborhood_replicates/overlap.csv)
