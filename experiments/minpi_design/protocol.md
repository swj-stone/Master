# Compensated MinPI development experiment

All six historical prepared sources at seed 42 and the existing seven-source forest study at seed 42 have previously been opened. Treat them as development data. For every arm use training-only transformations, the same unpenalized intercept, ridge penalty, voting universe and support checks. Evaluate a linear basis, then a separate three-term piecewise-linear basis per raw feature (knots at the one-third and two-thirds training quantiles). Do not choose the basis on these same outer outcomes and present it as confirmation.

The equal-size mixture game charges squared error under a common uniform mask of each cardinality. Refitting is permitted. At each size compare a shared set of coefficients to the optimal size-specific full-input linear head on the fitting sample. Compute the minimum vote across sizes as the negative maximum regret. The worst regret on that sample is attained at singleton or intact size. Fit one robust shared head using the exact one-dimensional dual, then delete the feature yielding least robust regret. A cheap alternative uses the lower bound at the current least favorable mixture; record its departures from the exact path. Strong controls are backward ridge on the intact problem, backward ridge under a 50:50 endpoint mixture, ordinary MDI and supervised marginal importance. All arms share a robust head or intact head when comparing their support rankings. Record timing including preprocessing, ranking and width checks. Report the ordinary intact head and robust model separately.

The smallest support returned on any path must preserve the two intact predictive scores on the independent acceptance sample against the corresponding full head (AUC/accuracy within .01; R² within .02 and MAE increase at most .02 fitting response standard deviations). For the robust readout also require worst normalized squared excess per available fraction relative to the *same full shared head*, across all sizes, at most .02 of acceptance null loss. This latter loss difference is affine in size for two fixed heads. The outer partition is accessed only after fitting and support selection. The ordinary-head comparison uses its own full reference and clean-score guard only. The previous XGBoost/MDI and gain-refit results were obtained with different learners and are external comparators. If the robust vote cannot improve over matched pooled/intact rule on these development sources, report that result rather than searching test labels for settings.
