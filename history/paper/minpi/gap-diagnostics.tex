\paragraph{Which simulation conditions transferred?}
In the $5/6$ simulation, the absolute size-16 marginal in~\eqref{eq:identity}, averaged across columns and rows, was 0.1820 on same-instrument and 0.0009 on cross-instrument comparisons (equal weight per world). The corresponding wrist--ankle training values were 0.1140 and 0.1025. The simulated nuisance block nearly silenced the cross-instrument rows at size 16. Under fair matching they still averaged only 0.0006, yet raw signs won: only 48.2\% of active votes on those rows were positive, versus 71.4\% at the biased policy and 47.8\% on MHEALTH (row-averaged). The direction of these votes affects $G_0$ even when their pivotal weight is negligible.

Second, from labelled fitting observations we computed each feature's cross-instrument covariance of class means, weighting classes equally and dividing by its pooled within-instrument variance. Negative terms accounted for 67.1\% of the total absolute covariance in simulation and 2.2\% on MHEALTH (1.7--2.5\% on omitting each training person). The simulated $G_{16}$ support averaged 5.88 of six features with opposing class profiles, versus 0.96 for the minimum. All four $G_{16}$ wrist--ankle features had positively aligned profiles. This diagnostic cannot identify a physical polarity change or establish that a reference was reliable. Gas measures chronological drift and materials holds out composition groups. Neither design identifies the simulated nuisance matching probability.

The consequence for selection was visible before prediction. Taking the minimum removed five or six of the six $G_{16}$ columns in every biased-acquisition world. On the primary gas, materials and MHEALTH ballots the lowest minimum among the $G_{16}$ selections still exceeded the highest outside minimum by 0.0023, 0.0006 and 0.0037. On those fits, changing the aggregation could not change a downstream model fitted with the same data, columns and seed. These retrospective diagnostics explain an observed support agreement. Their ability to forecast the quality of a changed support on a new source remains untested.
