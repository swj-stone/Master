\paragraph{Transfer between wrist and ankle.}
We specified a five-person training and five-person holdout on the existing MHEALTH recordings~\citep{banos2014mhealth}. From two-second windows we extracted 40 acceleration summaries. Of 192 wrist queries, 128 used left-ankle references and 64 used right-wrist references, all from other training participants. A shared logistic model used four selected features. Averaging each held-out person's lower wrist/ankle balanced accuracy gave 0.421 for the minimum and $G_{16}$ (identical supports), 0.463 for $G_0$, $G_4$ and the layer mean (another common support), 0.323 for $F$, 0.474 for random selection and 0.705 at full width. The five paired minimum-minus-sign differences in participant order were $-0.057,+0.005,-0.037,-0.020,-0.104$. The minimum had the higher mean weakest-layer score on held-out comparisons (0.0365 versus 0.0334).
