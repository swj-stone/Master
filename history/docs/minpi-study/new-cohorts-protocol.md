# Transfer on two new data sources

Frozen before downloading the archives or fitting the selectors. The two sources were chosen for their distinct evaluation units and because neither appeared in the earlier study. Gas discrimination under sensor drift tests a classification screen. Critical-temperature prediction on held-out compositions tests a regression screen, where the older five support changes were observed.

Use UCI Gas Sensor Array Drift (224), batches 1–7 for training and 8–10 for testing, retaining all 128 inputs. Give the three later batches equal weight and report each batch's recall averaged over the classes present in it. Use UCI Superconductivity (464), the 81 descriptors in train.csv, with critical temperature as outcome. Keep rows together when they share an identical descriptor vector or identical normalized elemental proportions (rounded to eight decimals). Join transitive matches. Apply GroupShuffleSplit(test_size=0.25, random_state=61429) once, grouping these connected components. Score row-weighted test R² and MAE. A paired percentile bootstrap on 2,000 samples of whole test components gives conditional uncertainty for R² differences. The three gas batches are too few to estimate transfer uncertainty across future batches.

In both tasks use K={0,4,16}, h=ceil(0.10p), and the published training-only neighbour builder. The primary selector seed is 1129. Seeds 2027 and 3469 measure algorithmic sensitivity on the same test population. The primary downstream fit is standardized ridge with the archived settings. Compare unweighted mean distance difference, mean signed vote, largest layer, minimum, reference geometry, full-training univariate F, and equal-width uniform selection, with a full-column reference. Use source column order to break ties. Store all predictions, supports, split indices and timing scopes.

The directional prediction is that the largest layer improves test performance over the mean signed vote when pivotally weighted training comparisons transfer. Call a predictive improvement practically material here at +0.01 balanced accuracy or +0.02 R². Evaluate the minimum separately against the largest layer with the same criteria. A surrogate improvement is an increase in the average coordinatewise minimum on held-out comparison rows for the selected support. Compute held-out votes from held-out rows only, using a separate seed (training seed + 10000), after all models and supports are fixed. Report disagreement or reversal across tasks; each source contributes one transfer experiment. No test-set tuning, seed selection or alternative budget search.
