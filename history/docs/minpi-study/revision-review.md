# Independent internal review of the revised pivotal minPI manuscript

The original review in [the paper directory](../../paper/minpi/README.md) concerned the 15-page manuscript. This review addresses the shorter revision and its newly released prediction records and implementation. I reviewed the matched and prospective comparisons, independently recalculated selected outcomes and tested the integer support code against literal subset enumeration. This is a simulated specialist review for the author.

## Findings on the original objections

| Question in the original review | Finding in the revision |
| --- | --- |
| Which budget decisions does the minimum actually change? | Five of ten retrospective regression splits changed, four on Communities and Crime and one on diabetes. I recovered every changed support and the twenty model-specific paired differences in test R² and MAE from the stored predictions. The other five splits had identical predictions. Only two changed supports improved ridge R², and one improved tree R². Direct reference-geometry ranking is a strong cheap control on these sources. |
| Does disagreement have predictive content? | Two prespecified comparison-generating regimes put measurement disagreement on either valid or reversed hit/miss assignments. I recovered all 288 world-by-method AUCs and held-out weakest-layer scores in the two initial regimes. The directions of the pivotal-weighting contrasts reverse. These are tests of supplied comparisons. The label-based nearest-neighbour finder in the default pipeline cannot be credited for the synthetic results. |
| Can the minimum outperform simpler scores on fresh observations? | With two simulated instruments and nuisance-biased acquisition, it can. The last simulation was designed after seeing the uniform-background experiment. It fixed four policies before generating fresh worlds and reported all four. Independent refits at the chosen 5/6 policy gave mean worst-instrument AUCs of 0.975 for the minimum, 0.779 for size four and 0.632 for the layer average. Using representative labelled fitting rows for univariate F screening gave 0.991. With unbiased background matching, the layer average also beat the minimum. These controls delimit the value of the minimum when the available feature evidence consists of acquired comparisons. |
| Does the improvement transfer to real measurements? | The primary size-16/minimum supports agreed on both Gas Sensor Drift and Superconductivity. I recomputed all 48 source/seed/method prediction rows and confirmed the rational minimum support on each of six vote matrices. Gas train/test batches are chronologically disjoint. An independent check of the two superconductivity input files found no identical descriptor vectors or matched normalized compositions across the boundary. In MHEALTH I verified disjoint people and windows, the training query/reference identities, and all 30 subject-by-method entries against saved predictions. The minimum and size 16 agree; both trail raw signs on the specified mean worse-location outcome. |
| What belongs to the finite-game calculation? | I checked the row-count identity, no-abstention rank-gap proof, coordinatewise decision objective, conditional query bound, and rational cutoff argument. The query bound fixes the reference geometry and counts the two related comparisons from one query as one sampling unit. It supplies no prediction-risk or population-variable recovery guarantee. The support guarantee uses strict exclusion, exact handling of unresolved columns and input order for ties. |
| What does exactness cost? | In 720 independent small-matrix checks, I compared the returned support with literal enumeration of every peer subset at all widths tested, across tied columns, all retention counts and four fixed-point precisions. The 4,096-column example has a gap of two divided by 908054843662451242401, which follows directly from a size-sixteen all-eight-negative-peer probability. Both the original many-way tie and the perturbed unique-winner construction selected the second column in integer arithmetic and the first in double precision. Only two columns needed exact fallback in the latter case. I reproduced the manuscript's wide-matrix medians from `integer-final/trials.csv`: 15.672 ms for the integer certificate, 108.642 ms for exhaustive integer scoring and 11.631 ms for the floating certificate. The nine prospective matrices and six retrospective real splits are distinct from the targeted counterexamples. |

## Revision queries and disposition

I requested two controls omitted from the polarity experiment, the intermediate layer and the layer average. Their retrospective status and the inexpensive F screen matter because the manufactured positive result could otherwise suggest a predictive advantage over all cheap alternatives. Both controls are in the revised manuscript. The independently reconstructed logistic predictions for the original minimum agreed with the archived probabilities. The common prediction population across acquisition policies explains the repeated F value. The manuscript identifies each additional policy as part of a follow-up and gives the earlier negative result.

The scope of the numerical claim is a finite voting game. Agreement with exhaustive double precision alone would leave a gap in the numerical argument, which the new integer method and counterexample resolve. The explicit fixed-vote timing scope is essential. A speed advantage relative to exhaustive integer scoring on one wide matrix supplies no speed guarantee for full model fitting or another stakeholder's hardware. An independent run of `lean Pivotal.lean` completed with the profile, pivotal-increment, prefix, dyadic enclosure and penalty-flip lemmas checked. The full implementation and the statistical claims remain outside the formalization.

I would support external scientific review as a methods paper on certified fixed-budget voting scores and the limits of the scores as predictive filters. The machine-precision failure is reproducible, the component-wise interventions can fail, and the two domains where literal pivotal weighting helped or harmed are explained without merging their targets. A broad promise of better generic feature selection would still lack support. The synthetic polarity and acquisition mechanisms were deliberately constructed, their frequency in applications is unknown, MHEALTH has five test participants, and the new material study covers a single source. A positive general-purpose accuracy or operational-speed verdict would require new external evidence. Novelty and priority remain editorial judgments.

## Final manuscript check

I reviewed all ten pages of [the ICOMP-format manuscript](../../paper/minpi/main.pdf), SHA-256 `823c1bb5279af6eb113f01ccbfe02141d2c227e6d2cfc48b64a16962b138a3ec`. The three tables, figure, five numbered theoretical results, discussion, availability statement, AI declaration and nineteen references are present. The figure and tables are legible, the equations stay within their columns, and all extracted word boxes lie inside the page boundaries. The final LaTeX log has two vertical-spacing messages and no overfull box, missing character or unresolved citation. The discussion distinguishes the 0.94 and 0.97 training-to-support timing ratios from the fixed-vote timings. The four supplementary G4, layer-mean, F and random comparisons are identified as diagnostics or clearly described apart from the original primary contrasts. The final software run recorded 1,482 passes and four test skips owing to Windows symbolic-link permissions. The manuscript corrections raised in this review are closed for this PDF.
