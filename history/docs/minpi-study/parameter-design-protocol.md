# Parameter choices and their evidence

This is a retrospective index of the settings in the released experiments. The linked protocols record which choices preceded each run. Sample sizes, grids, learner settings and practical margins are design choices. The mathematical guarantee is conditional on a supplied vote matrix, peer counts, budget and tie rule. It requires no distributional assumptions or predictive tuning.

## Exact selection on a fixed matrix

| Setting | Meaning, choice and evidence |
|---|---|
| Votes and target | Rows carry ternary votes. For each feature average its signed marginal contribution over rows and uniform subsets of exactly $k$ other features, then take the minimum over a nonempty set of integer peer counts $0\leq k<p$. The smaller input index breaks an exact tie. These choices define the target. Permuting features at a mathematical tie may change which tied feature is retained. |
| Peer counts $K$ | Default $\{0,4,16\}$, capped at $p-1$ and deduplicated. Zero measures the unweighted sign vote. Four and sixteen sample two short committee sizes fourfold apart. Explicit user counts must be feasible. Among 17 measured panel/budget pairs, using only 0, 4 or 16 changed 10, 11 or 6 supports relative to the default. All-odd $\{1,5,15\}$ changed five. Adding 1 changed one. These are different games with no empirically optimal $K$ established. [Design](precision-design-protocol.md) · [Evidence](../../results/minpi-study/precision-design/peer-set-summary.csv) |
| Retained width $h$ | Any integer $1\leq h\leq p$. The original $\lceil0.1p\rceil$ gives comparable compression across selectors. We tested $1,\lceil0.1p\rceil,\lceil0.5p\rceil$ on six real matrices and two constructed matrices. Gas with $h=64/128$ and added layer 1 exposed a floating tie-order discrepancy. At 10% on the original 42 measured panels the two arithmetics agreed. [Exact cutoffs and supports](../../results/minpi-study/precision-design/targets.csv) |
| First layer | Any chosen layer is an upper bound on the minimum. Strict pruning must retain equality unless stable tie precedence is established. The largest-layer default followed earlier observations of frequent support agreement. Comparing anchors on the original 42 measured panels gave median fractions requiring secondary layers of 0.385, 0.289 and 0.103 for the three ascending sizes. These are operation counts. [Anchor protocol](anchor-design-protocol.md) |
| Integer mode and $b$ | With $D=\operatorname{lcm}_{k\in K}\binom{p-1}{k}$, $wD\leq2^{63}-1$ suffices for the released signed 64-bit implementation. Otherwise dyadic lower and upper bounds precede exact integer resolution of the remaining boundary. The default is the smaller of 40 bits and the safe accumulator cap. Forcing $b=0,8,20,40$ spans a unit grid, $1/256$, about $10^{-6}$ (close to the narrowest positive original measured gap), and about $10^{-12}$. All 552 configurations agreed with exhaustive and independently counted scores. Measured targets with final exact comparisons numbered 83, 48, 4 and 4 out of 102. No outcome determined the dispatch threshold. [Native-word protocol](word-design-protocol.md) · [Precision evidence](../../results/minpi-study/precision-design/findings.md) |

## Constructing training comparisons

| Setting | Implementation and sensitivity |
|---|---|
| Sampling and labels | Cap at 256 training observations to limit dense neighbour search. Use disjoint reference/query banks. Balance classification sampling over classes subject to availability. For regression sample rows uniformly and define five response bands from reference quintiles, merging coincident cuts. A hit shares the label/band and a miss differs. The two rows per query are dependent. Increasing the cap to 512 gave default-support Jaccards 0.188, 0.529 and 0 on Gisette, gas and materials. Using three or seven response bands retained two of nine default materials features in each case. |
| Geometry and ranks | Standardize in float32 on reference rows, use scale one for SD $\leq10^{-7}$, replace missing standardized entries with zero and clip to $[-4,4]$. For $g$ groups subtract $(g-1)/\max(1,n_R-1)$ from the between/total variance share and truncate below zero. Mix the normalized shares with uniform weights in an 80:20 ratio. All-zero shares give uniform geometry. Take first and fourth nearest hits/misses, capping each rank at availability. The per-coordinate vote is the sign of absolute miss distance minus absolute hit distance. Uniform geometry gave Jaccards 0.225, 0.857 and 0.286. Ranks (1,8) gave 0.495, 0.368 and 0.286 on the same three sources. |

The geometric constants, clipping, rank pair and sample cap are inherited engineering choices for one source of ballots. The one-factor study also includes cap 128, supervised weight 1 and ranks (1,1). It uses training seed 1129, three sources and no prediction-based tuning. The score concentration calculation conditions on the reference and independent queries, with effective count $n_Q$ rather than $2n_Q$. The finite training replay samples without replacement. ## What the numerical timings vary

- **Fixed matrices.** Widths 128/4,096/16,384, row counts 32/192, abstention probabilities 0/0.75 and seeds 34123/34127/34129 produce 36 matrices using Gaussian row and column shifts with logistic sign noise. Budgets 2/10/50% compare a small initial prefix, the original budget and a wide boundary. Warm up once, then randomize five single-thread rounds per matrix and summarize paired medians. Additional integer matrices use seeds 38119/38149 and six archived real panels. Three-row near ties use $p=128,512,4096$ and $h=1$ to isolate arithmetic at the cutoff. The separate sampling comparison tests 4/16/64/256 draws per nonempty layer on widths 64 through 16,384 and abstention 0.1/0.8. [Frontier](frontier-protocol.md) · [Integer](integer-protocol.md) · [Sampling](kernel-protocol.md)
- **Training-to-support time.** Regenerate ballots from in-memory training arrays on each call, starting with derived windows for MHEALTH. Exclude file loading, split creation and raw-signal windowing. The raw-cost experiment separates selection from fitting and prediction on the returned columns. The native-word study measures identical paths once per round. Both use a warm-up, five randomized single-thread rounds and panelwise median ratios. Ordering seeds are 411217 and 91831, respectively. The precision study starts with fixed votes, forces dyadic scoring and uses three rounds (order seed 44317). Of 47 archived panels, 42 are measured, five are artificial Madelon splits. Repeated splits share observations. [Raw cost](raw-cost-protocol.md) · [Native-word comparison](word-design-protocol.md) · [Precision design](precision-design-protocol.md)

## Simulated reference reliability

Each world has 60 shuffled channels. A six-column budget lets either signal block fill the support, while 48 shared-background channels can dominate the majority. Draw 192 training and 768 independent assessment triplets, plus 384 separate labelled fitting and 1,536 test observations per world. Use a common standardized L2 logistic model ($C=1$) within each support. Unit Gaussian errors set the noise scale. These are fixed simulation sizes without a documented power calculation. Intervals resample whole independent worlds and retain the pairing of selectors.

| Experiment | Intervention, endpoint and uncertainty |
|---|---|
| Valid/reversed assignments | Six class and six site loadings are uniform on [0.65,1.25], background loadings on [1.5,2.5]. Half of the assignments reverse the hit and miss in expectation. Two regimes exchange which assignment has the aligned background. There are 48 independent worlds per regime, entropy (20261117, regime, world). Compare $G_{16}$ and $G_0$ by test AUC, with a 0.01 practical margin. Two primary contrasts use 97.5% two-sided paired percentile intervals, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261118. [Mechanism](mechanism-protocol.md) |
| Instrument polarity | Six stable loadings are uniform on [0.55,0.85], six stronger reversing loadings on [1.30,1.70], and 48 background loadings on [1.50,2.50]. Hit labels remain correct. One third of reference pairs share the query instrument and background. Otherwise the other instrument reverses six channels and one reference matches the background. Its test prevalence is 2/3, independent of class. Forty-eight worlds (entropy 20261119) test whether the minimum improves the assessment weakest-layer score and the lower of the two test-instrument AUCs. Two primary comparisons against $G_{16}$ use 97.5% intervals and thresholds 0 and 0.01, with 10,000 world resamples, seed 20261120. [Calibration](calibration-protocol.md) |
| Acquisition bias | Follow-up fixed after fair matching: vary the probability that the hit matches the background in cross-instrument triplets over 1/2, 2/3, 5/6 (primary) and 1. The same 48 new worlds (entropy 20261121) and prediction data are shared across policies. The two endpoints crossed with $G_0$ and $G_{16}$ give four primary contrasts. Bonferroni yields individual two-sided 98.75% intervals, using 10,000 paired world resamples, seed 20261122. These deliberately imposed frequencies do not estimate real reference accuracy. [Bias protocol](acquisition-bias-protocol.md) · [Transfer diagnostics](../../results/minpi-study/transfer-gap/findings.md) |

## Held-out predictive checks

| Data | Split, metric, models and seeds |
|---|---|
| Historical eight-source benchmark | Five 75/25 row splits, stratified for classification, with seeds 1129, 2027, 3301, 4003, 5009. Seed 391 permutes column order for all arms. Standardized LSQR ridge uses $\alpha=1$, tolerance 0.001 and at most 250 iterations. ExtraTrees uses 48 trees and square-root split candidates. Report AUC (macro one-vs-rest for multiclass), accuracy, or regression $R^2$ and MAE. Different seeds can contain the same people or rows. ISOLET splits are not speaker-blocked. The early descriptive margins, synthetic controls, pilot seed and one-hour case limit are in the [original config](../../configs/minpi-study.json) and [main protocol](main-experiment.md). |
| Gas and materials | Gas trains on batches 1–7 and gives batches 8–10 equal weight in balanced accuracy, using classes present in each batch. Materials keeps identical descriptors or normalized composition (eight decimals) together under transitive closure and holds out 25% of groups (split seed 61429). Report row-weighted $R^2$ and MAE, with 2,000 paired draws of whole held-out groups for a conditional 95% $R^2$ difference interval (seed 18617). Both use the same standardized ridge, primary training seed 1129 and sensitivity seeds 2027/3469 on the unchanged holdout. Practical margins are 0.01 balanced accuracy and 0.02 $R^2$. Separate assessment ballots use seed +10000. Three gas batches do not support precise future-batch inference. [Transfer protocol](new-cohorts-protocol.md) |
| MHEALTH acquisition | Pool wrist/ankle training windows from people 1–5, hold out 6–10. Build 40 summaries from nonoverlapping 100-sample windows at 50 Hz. Up to 16 wrist queries per activity give 192 training comparisons, 64 with wrist and 128 with ankle references from other training people. Here references use nearest standardized unweighted Euclidean distance on the assigned device. Fit one standardized L2 logistic model ($C=1$, L-BFGS, 2,000 iterations) using one device per training window, ankle probability 2/3. Select four columns. Average the smaller device-specific balanced accuracy within each of five people. The prespecified transfer criterion was a mean gain above 0.01 and a gain for at least four people. SeedSequence entropy 20261123. [Device protocol](device-protocol.md) |
| Additional held-out archives | Arcene/Dexter use their official labelled validation sets once, column permutation 391 and training seeds 1129/2039/3049, standardized L2 logistic ($C=1$) and paired class-stratified bootstrap of fixed-fit AUC differences (2,000 draws, seed 2718). UCI HAR uses the official 7,352/2,947 subject-disjoint split. Average balanced accuracy equally over nine held-out people. Seeds 17/29/43 vary training procedures on that split, with budgets 1/5/10/25/50%, standardized ridge ($\alpha=1$) or Euclidean 5-NN.
