# Literature and claim boundaries for the triplet voting study

## Related work for the manuscript

Relief scores a feature by comparing a query with nearby examples that share or differ in its label. ReliefF and RReliefF develop this principle for classification and regression. The proposed filter inherits its local distance comparisons from this family. Each comparison supplies a ternary ballot. A feature receives credit when its ballot changes the majority in a uniformly chosen coalition of a prescribed size. The empirical comparison that isolates this change uses exactly the same triplets and the same number of selected columns. The mean of the signed ballots is a matched ablation. Ordinary Relief updates also use the magnitude of the distance difference. [Kira and Rendell](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1992/AAAI92-020.pdf) · [Robnik-Šikonja and Kononenko](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1025667309714)

Cooperative-game selectors differ in what a coalition is asked to predict. Cohen, Dror and Ruppin estimate feature contributions to a predictive performance measure. ShapG uses a correlation graph to guide the approximation of a global Shapley attribution. Shi and Zhao take minimum marginal changes in a thresholded model-utility game, in which a negative marginal records an observed quota crossing. Here the payoff is the majority sign on a fixed training triplet. For each coalition size, the expected increment is a cardinality-specific semivalue on the game averaged over the triplets. The reported score takes the minimum of these size-specific increments. This construction permits exact coalition integration conditional on the sampled triplets. Predictive accuracy after refitting remains an empirical outcome. [Cohen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.2007.19.7.1939) · [ShapG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00506v2) · [Shi and Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33885) · [Dubey et al.](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.6.1.122)

Minimum aggregation has several precedents. Fleuret selects variables that retain conditional mutual information given each previously selected feature. Zheng and Raskutti minimize conditional importance across conditioning sets and characterize redundancy under null monotonicity. The triplet rule minimizes three designated coalition-size averages. Even at the same size, coalition effects of opposite signs across training triplets can cancel. Its output orders columns for a prespecified retention budget. [Fleuret](https://jmlr.org/papers/v5/fleuret04a.html) · [MinShap v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15107v2)

## Algebra and a concrete limitation

For fixed training triplets, write the feature votes on row r as v_rj. For each feature and cardinality k, symmetry under permutations of peers of the same vote leaves only the counts of positive, negative and zero peers. A positive focal vote changes sign utility exactly when the peer margin is -1 or 0. A negative vote changes it exactly when the margin is 0 or 1. The three-color hypergeometric distribution gives the probability of either event by summing over feasible triples of counts. Within a row and a size, the resulting marginal has at most three values. Defining A and B as the pivotal probabilities for a positive and a negative focal vote respectively gives the identity (A+B)v_rj/2 + (A-B)|v_rj|/2. An absent sign class may be assigned probability zero in this expression. When every vote is nonzero, the last term is a row intercept shared by every feature, so each size ranks columns by reweighted signed triplet votes. This explains what can change relative to the plain score and identifies information lost after signing.

For example, if every feature votes +1 on every triplet, the empty-coalition marginal is 1 for every feature and every positive-size marginal is 0. The minimum is therefore 0 for every feature whenever a positive size is present, although any one feature wins all these triplets on its own. Distinct predictive variables that induce identical vote columns receive identical scores. A top-k boundary tie needs a documented rule. Resampling triplets introduces a further source of variation even though coalition integration is exact. The same query can produce several triplets, and queries can reuse a reference example. Their row count cannot serve as the sample size for an independent-observations confidence interval.

## Scope and evidence handoff

- Compression at a prescribed retained fraction is an experimental condition. Compare utility and total time at matched width, and report the full-width model separately. For a prefilter claim, pair the same selector with and without screening and include both stages of elapsed time.
- Compare the minimum, the same-size mean, and the singleton empty-coalition score on unchanged triplets. Enumerate every coalition on tiny games that contain ties. A Monte Carlo comparison addresses numerical accuracy or cost on the same game. A comparison with the archived method addresses the entire pipeline.
- Exactness refers to the finite surrogate conditional on triplets and selected sizes. The proposed cardinalities 0, 4 and 16 are clipped and deduplicated by input width. On wide matrices these are small peer sets. Report the precise set in place of small, medium and large fractions of the full width.
- Validate feature ranking separately from prediction using signed-vote recovery, matched downstream fits, or a planted-support case where ground truth exists. Interacting and duplicate predictors and datasets with limited signal give informative adverse cases.
- Repeated random splits within a dataset can share observations. Provide per-dataset results and matched splits. Evidence about repeatability of a score differs from evidence about stability of the selected support across resamples. If the latter is claimed, show it directly. [Nogueira et al.](https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/17-514.html) · [Demšar](https://jmlr.org/papers/v7/demsar06a.html)
- Gisette and Madelon belong to the NIPS feature-selection competition. State the source partitions, feature widths, and resampling procedure actually used. Challenge leaderboard scores used the organizers' withheld labels and protocol. [Guyon et al.](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2004/hash/5e751896e527c862bf67251a474b3819-Abstract.html)
- The snapshot contains several predecessors. The conference paper describes thresholded refit utility. PIv1 combines TreeSHAP-based stratum votes with model rankings and a validated subset search. The active selector in the snapshot uses a Gain anchor and deletion votes at three masked contexts. The compact triplet filter is a separate predecessor and provides the hit/miss construction used here. Name the implemented historical comparator precisely. Historical performance numbers belong to their original protocols.
