# Conference extension on Credit Card Fraud

Credit Card Fraud is a separate transfer source from the frozen ICOMP archive. The original classifier was XGBoost. This comparison holds the earlier row split and feature schema fixed and fits the same standardized LSQR ridge classifier used in the numerical minPI comparisons. Before inspecting its results we selected the archived seed 42 split, the first of five. The immutable raw CSV in the thesis checkout is verified against the original 148,701,764-byte snapshot and SHA-256. All three row lists are checked against their recorded digests and must partition the raw data. The labels and the standardized values of all 29 train and test columns are checked against the frozen CSVs. The 16% validation portion is unused. Fitting uses the 64% training portion, learning scaling inside the timed downstream pipeline, and prediction uses the 20% test portion. Full predictions must agree with an independent ridge fit on the original prepared files.

New minPI, previous minPI, Full, same-width F and a single uniform random subset each run five timed rotations per independent single-threaded process. All arms use the same unweighted classifier and its ordinary zero decision threshold. Report ROC AUC, average precision, fraud recall and detected positives over held-out positives at that threshold. Timing includes selection, slicing, scaling, fitting and prediction; scoring and the historical input audit are outside timing. A single split describes conditional performance on that test population.
