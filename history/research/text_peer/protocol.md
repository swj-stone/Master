# Text minPI confirmation protocol

The primary candidate uses class-balanced, leave-one-document-out smoothed word evidence, logistic payoff, document length square-root normalization, strata zero and all other vocabulary columns, and auto width equal to the number of strictly positive worst-stratum gains (tolerance 1e-15, minimum one). Both strata use the same fixed normalization for a given document. Selecting the full vocabulary fails compression. Method and budgets freeze before scoring any confirmation outcomes. Choice used three deterministic inner holdouts from the historical first-fold training rows of SMS, Amazon, IMDb, and Yelp. Among the two best development candidates by BA (unit and square-root log), square-root beat both its single-stratum controls at matched width. Unit lost to the all-peer-only control. Additional penalties and bounded score utilities were inferior on those training holdouts. These were iterative development decisions.

All 17 existing UCI outer folds and Turkish SMS/mail three folds per source are evaluated as historical rechecks. The archived SpamAssassin acquisition test is one additional historical test. Two fresh Cornell text sources are fixed before evaluation: polarity v2.0 full-length movie reviews (movie_reviews via NLTK) and subjective/objective sentences v1.0 (subjectivity via NLTK). Three stratified folds, seed 39191, unique complete token bags, train-only vocabulary, shared removal of constant terms and train/test identical projected bags. The latter corpus contains subjective film-review snippets, so generalization between these English movie domains is limited. Both new corpora are reported even if accuracy worsens. Comparison uses full, existing native document vote, F, pooled univariate ridge gain, uniform random, document frequency and two single-stratum ablations, all local methods using explicitly named dispatch. Half-width F/pooled/random included as independent deployable baselines. All non-full methods use identical standardization, RidgeClassifier LSQR and prediction pipeline. Primary joint vs full follows the established BA gap >= -0.02 and faster measured total with true deletion. Report results for each source and changes against identical-width controls. For speed, paired timing charged from prepared binary CSR inputs to predictions, one-thread rotating methods, across two processes; shared tokenization excluded. No tested source is silently removed from scope. No claim across source families solely from a favorable fold.
